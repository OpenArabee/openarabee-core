# Contributor agreement (DRAFT, not in force)

**Status: draft of 2026-11-15, written without legal review. It binds nobody yet.** It will
be reviewed in the legal consultation foreseen in charter §6 and put into force by the
project owner before the first external contribution is merged (charter §8, D-002).

## Purpose

OpenArabee publishes its code under the MIT licence and its data under CC BY 4.0. So that
the project can keep doing so, can correct licensing mistakes, and can offer its work in
other forms later (a book, apps, datasets), the project owner needs a clear permission from
everyone whose work goes into it.

## Draft terms

1. **You keep your rights.** You remain the author of your contribution and may use it in
   any way you like.
2. **Licence to the project owner.** You grant Obada Obaid, as owner of OpenArabee, a
   worldwide, perpetual, irrevocable, non-exclusive, royalty-free licence to use, reproduce,
   modify, publish and distribute your contribution, to license it to others under any
   terms, and to transfer these permissions to a successor of the project.
3. **Public licences.** Your contribution is in any case published under the licences of
   this repository: code and schema under MIT, everything else under CC BY 4.0.
4. **Your assurances.** The contribution is your own work, or you have the right to submit
   it. It is not copied from a work or database that forbids this. Every value you add names
   a source from the project's source register, and you have checked it there yourself.
   If you used an AI system to prepare it, you say so and you have checked the result
   against the named source.
5. **Credit.** The project names its contributors.
6. **No obligation.** The project need not accept or keep a contribution.
