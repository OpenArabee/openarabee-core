## The 35 abwāb of *Matn al-Bināʾ*

First transcribed on 2026-11-15 from a typed reading copy (takw.in, pp. 2-15), then collated entry by entry on 2026-11-16 against the page images of the printed edition listed under source 'bina' (Majmūʿ muhimmāt al-mutūn, 4th printing, Cairo 1369/1949, pp. 557-568). All 35 patterns, model verbs, maṣdars, statements of use and examples agree with the print, except: the print confirms افعيلالا for bāb 18 (the reading copy has a slip), and it writes the maṣdar of bāb 7 with hamza. Page references are to the printed edition; group labels use the wording of the text. Western equivalents were checked on 2026-11-16 against the page images of Wright vol. I (§35, §§66-72). Spelling is normalised as described in docs/data-model.md §4 (no case endings on maṣdars, hamzat al-waṣl written اِ, no sukūn on long vowels). Examples: vowel marks kept on the verb only. The first six abwāb give no maṣdar because the text gives none.
